About Us

Our NGO is dedicated to uniting the Ijaw race through cultural preservation, community empowerment, and shared identity. We believe that by strengthening our roots, we can uplift humanity. Through advocacy, education, and humanitarian outreach, we work to build bridges across communities, promote peace, and create opportunities for a better future.

Our Projects

We run impactful initiatives to improve lives and empower communities. Here are some of our key projects:

Community Education

Providing educational resources and training programs to underserved communities.

Members Monthly Meetings

Meetings are held at our office every last sunday of the month for members sensitization and unity.

Operation feed the people

Providing food supplies, healthy food awareness, and promoting growing local food crops.

×

Community Education

Our Community Education Project focuses on empowering youth and adults through literacy programs, vocational training, and mentorship. We collaborate with local schools and volunteers to improve educational outcomes in underserved areas.

×

Members monthy meetings

Through this project, we provide sustainable solutions as to regards community conflicts, sensitizing members about the impact of unity among ijaws and other tribes.

×

Healthcare Support

Our Healthcare Support initiative distributes essential medicines, organizes free medical camps, and conducts health awareness sessions in rural communities.
