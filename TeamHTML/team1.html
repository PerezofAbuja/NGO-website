Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei

Founder and President, IBW

BIOGRAPHY AMB. JACK WHIGGY OGEI, is an indigene of Delta and Bayelsa states in Nigeria, with paternal roots in Toru-Tamigbe town, Burutu Local Government Area (LGA) of Delta state and maternal roots in Aleibiri town, Ekeremor LGA of Bayelsa state.

He was born in Toru-Tamigbe town in the year 1986 August 17th into the noble family of Prince, Jack Eyoro-ere Ogei of blessed memories. He began his primary education in 1994 in his home town Toru-Tamigbe and meritoriously obtained his First School Leaving Certificate (FSLC) in the year2000. He immediately began his secondary education in the year 2001 in Toru-Tamigbe town too. Shortly, he relocated to Bomadi -overside, Bomadi town in Delta state to unite with his mum and maternal siblings. At this time his mum and dad where no longer together. While in Bomadi he continued is secondary education in ESENA-EBE grammar school Bomadi.

Nevertheless, in the year 2003 his mother Mrs Ngozi Augustina Eyeki, relocated to Yenagoa, the capital city of Bayelsa state in search of a better life. Therefore ambitious Whiggy was bonding with his grandma and other maternal siblings. However, in the year 2005 he also relocated to Yenagoa for a better life, where he observed a little recession on his schooling due to lack of finance. But his unquenchable desire for learning and wanting to get educated kept on agonizing him until he finally rejuvenated his learning ambition again.

This time around he got admitted into Central Secondary School Opolo-epie Yenagoa Bayelsa state. And subsequently obtained his National Examinations Councils (NECO) certificate in the year 2009. As a self sponsor, while schooling he engaged into several jobs to stay afloat financially. Some of those menial jobs were: cart/wheelbarrow pushing (carry-carry), farm clearing, a flunky, etc, he was indeed a hustler who knows his roots and was prudent with his earnings.

During this time he was battling with the challenge of putting up with all the insults of his former classmate and friend whom he was staying with in Fambge town, Bayelsa state. One day before Whiggy could return back home from his usual daily hustles his friend had emptied the property of the room including a few clothes and documents belonging to Jack Whiggy Ogei, and took french-leave out of the town.

Unfortunately, the left alone Whiggy never knew that the room rent was due for repayment over four months. Shortly after two weeks owner of the apartment evacuated poor Whiggy out of the room for inability of rent renewal. Whiggy became homeless but, refused to join his mum who was a few miles away. Rather, he moved in with a friend, this time, to a bigger city so as to start afresh. Not too long, he started a job in a Private Hospital at Kpansia in Yenagoa as an assistant porter. A few months later he quitted the job and joined himself with a Private Security Company called Erekabo Security Services LTD. owned by Mr. Anthony Sawyer (Rtd AIG) of blessed memories. After a short while he was appointed a supervisor due to his humility and hard work. Six months later he was yet appointed as the bursar/accountant (pay master) of a branch of the company due to trust and sincerity he possessed as a quality. In the year 2009 the company ventured into renting of party items business where Whiggy was also appointed as the manager.

In the year 2010 he decided to further is education through the mercy and grace of God almighty by himself. He gained admission into the Niger Delta University (NDU) Wilberforce Island, Amassoma Bayelsa state. Where he studied Accountancy. However, in the year 2011 he lost his father. But his journey to get educated was never aborted, although it was very tough for him to keep pace with learning due to the challenge of hustling and schooling. He said "several times before I could resume class other students have finished writing test". This experience affected is grades and academic performance, but he never retreated.

Finally however, in the year 2014 he completed his Bachelor degree in financial accounting and was called up for National Youths Service Corps (NYSC) . He was posted to his primary place of assignments at Institute of Continuous Education (ICE) in Ejedkota Ughelli, Delta State, where he served as a classroom financial accounting teacher. In August 2015, he meritoriously completed this national duty as a corp member with an honour of Discharge Certificate (DC). Expressly, after his NYSC completion he reunited with his long time employer Erekabo Security Services Ltd. This time he was appointed as the General Security Manager (GSM) Ughelli Delta state branch. He operated in this capacity for four years. In his tenure the company recorded a tremendous growth and expansion.

However, in September 2019 he pened down his resignation letter with three months in lieu of notice to enable the company to scout for a suitable replacement. And finally he left his employer in December 2019. He then, registered his own Cleaning Company called WID ROYAL CLEANING SERVICES in the year 2021. Which is involved in industrial and domestic professional cleaning services located at Otovwodo, Ughelli Delta state. Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei, is also the CEO and the chairman of FRONT BATTLE SECURITY SERVICES LTD. A rapidly growing security company with a high level of professionalism. He is a shareholder of Wid Royal Snacks, Bakes and confectionaries. He is also the founder and the current president general of Ijaw Brotherhood Empowerment Initiative, popularly known and called as IJAW BROTHERHOOD WORLDWIDE aka Elite Group of Ijaw Ethnicity on their Facebook page and website.

In the year 2025, after meritoriously completing his training/studies and other requirements, the Institute of Global Peace & Conflict Management inducted and certified him with award/honour of a Distinguished Ambassador on Global Peace & Security Consultancy. Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei' s professionalism and passion for global peace through mediation and protection of lives and property is immeasurable. One of our interviews with him he said, his quest for the best modality/method to safeguard lives and property and to mediate for tranquility so as to bring about societal peace has just began. Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei, is also involved in several Ijaw cultural initiatives to breed peace, equality, transparency and a suitable progress to the Ijaw people and the global society. Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei is a philanthropist. A bonafide Ijaw son who is also considered a pioneer or co-founder of Ijaw Language Clinic. He also founded Facebook groups like Mass Literacy for the Ijaw Child, My Missing Rib and Victims of Love. Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei is also an investor, a writer, a motivational speaker and a preacher of moral values. He is indeed, the peoples' person.

In the year 2025 he was given a traditional title called THE AZAGADA 1 of Ijaw land by his friends, peer group and business associates. This traditional title means the following: Honourable, the Noble One, the Wise One and others refer to it as The Boss. Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei is indeed an epitome of sincerity, a proponent of unity and progress. Advocating for solidarity and enlightenment among the Ijaw people and the global society at large. Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei, is legally married to Mrs. Precious Ware-ebi Whiggy with five lovely kids. The couple now reside in Ughelli Delta state. Long Live Amb. Jack Whiggy Ogei Amen!!!!
